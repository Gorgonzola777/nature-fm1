**NATURE 1.1**: generative nature soundscapes for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Wind, sea, rain, fire, birds, crickets, wind chimes and thunder, synthesised in real time and changing by themselves,
with a full-screen pixel-art scene that follows the sound. [Try it in your browser](https://gorgonzola777.github.io/nature-fm1/).

**In this release**
- A NATURE splash at power-on (1.5 s): the name, what it is, the version.
- Smoother screen: the scene moves by the clock, and turning knobs no longer slows it.

**Since 1.0** the same features: 11 places on the white keys, 5 layers to switch, 11 events on the black keys (press
harder: stronger), pages for effects (reverb, echo, chorus, drive), LO-FI (crush, rate, wobble, dust), a DJ filter,
an LFO, fades and glide, wind chimes in a key and scale, TOUR (it plays itself), a sleep timer, SAVE for power-on.

**Install**: `nature-1.1.fwsc` below, with M-Flasher or `tools/fm1_install.py`; or from The Firmware Hub once listed.
Identity `FM-1_628311`. Built from source on GitHub (attested by zvenson/fm1-build). Older and newer FM-1s alike.
